# yt-chapters

Use the supplied SRT, VTT, or Whisper JSON transcript and sibling `chapters.py`. Preserve timestamp evidence; never invent boundaries. Require a 00:00 first chapter, at least three chapters, and ten seconds minimum per chapter. Draft labels in the transcript language, including Telugu. Treat generated titles as drafts and show validation status. This skill does not publish.
